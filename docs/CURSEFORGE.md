## Description

**WoW Translate turns chat in other languages into yours, right in your chat window, and can translate what you write back.**

Someone whispers you in Russian, trade chat is full of Chinese, your new guildmate only speaks Spanish? WoW Translate has you covered.

### Two ways to translate

**Quick mode (free)** works the moment you log in. No internet, no account, nothing to set up. It gives you the general meaning of a message, phrase by phrase. It's great for trade chat, group calls and short messages, but it isn't always accurate.

**Accurate mode** gives you real, natural translations. It needs the free WoW Translate Companion app on your PC and an account with a translation service (DeepL or Google give you a free amount every month, then charge you). Addons can't use the internet, which is why this part runs in a small app next to the game.

### Features

- Translates English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Chinese and Japanese chat
- Answers each player in their own language (optional)
- Item, quest and spell links shown with names in your language
- Hover the [T] tag to see the original message
- Pick which channels to translate: whispers, party, raid, guild, trade and more
- Shows how to read player names written in other alphabets
- The addon itself is available in 9 languages
- Light and fast: dictionaries load only when a language actually appears in your chat

### Getting started

Just install it and log in. Type **/wt** to open the options.

### Good to know

- During boss fights and rated matches, Blizzard hides other players' chat from all addons, so those lines aren't translated until the fight ends.
- The WoW: Forever beta forgets addon settings when you restart the game. Tick "Remember my settings after restart" in /wt to keep them.

Full guide, including Accurate mode setup: https://github.com/sanjaygbhat/wow-translate
