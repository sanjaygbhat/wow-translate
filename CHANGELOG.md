# WoW Translate 3.0.0 — WoW: Forever edition

The first version made for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

**New**
- **Quick mode (free):** translates chat right inside the game, with no internet and no setup. Covers English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Chinese (simplified and traditional) and Japanese, with about 14,000 gaming phrases and words.
- **Accurate mode:** natural, sentence-level translations through DeepL, Google or an OpenAI-compatible service, shown in the new WoW Translate Companion app (Windows).
- **Answer people in their language:** "Their language (auto)" replies to each player in the language they last wrote in.
- **Item, quest and spell links** are shown with their names in your language, straight from the game's own data.
- **[T] tag** on translated lines: hover to see the original, click to print it.
- **Latin spelling of foreign player names** in tooltips (uses the game's built-in transliteration).
- **The addon speaks your language:** German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Chinese.
- New options window, minimap button (right-click toggles translation) and addons-menu entry.
- **Remember my settings** option to work around the beta bug that forgets addon settings on restart.

**Changed**
- No DLL any more. The vanilla 1.12 edition, which needed one, is kept in `legacy/vanilla`.
- Chat that Blizzard locks during boss fights and rated matches is left untouched instead of erroring.
